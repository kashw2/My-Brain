### Severities

1. Superficial
2. Partial Thickness
3. Full Thickness

### Rule of Nines

Used to estimate the percentage of burns a person has suffered

### Signs and Symptoms

- Red, blistered, white or blackened skin
- Straw coloured liquid (plasma) oozing from blisters
- Pain
- Severe [[Cardiac Emergencies#Shock|Shock]]
- Altered consciousness

### Management

- Call 000
- Cool with clean water
- > 20 mins thermal, radiation or chemical burns
- > 30 mins, bitumen burns

> [!warning] **NEVER** let casualty shiver

- Cover with burn dressing, damp dressing or cling wrap

#### Chemical Burns

- Control danger / remove hazard
- Immediately dilute the chemical by irrigating affected area with cool running water for one hour
- Use appropriate PPE
- If shivering, cease irrigation and manage appropriately

#### Electrical Burns

- Always switch off power
- If conscious - control danger, cool burnt areas
- Cover impacted area with sterile non fluffy dressing or cling wrap
- Call 000
- If unconscious - control danger, clear [[Assess for life threatening injuries#Airways|Airways]], follow [[Responding to an Emergency Situation#Basic Life Support|Basic Life Support]] flow chart, [[Resuscitation#CPR Cycle|CPR Cycle]] f required

#### Inhalation Burns

**Recognition**

- Difficulty swallowing
- Difficulty breathing
- Pain to injured area
- Smoke or soot around mouth / nose

**Conscious**

- Reassure
- Remove constrictive clothing
- Administer oxygen therapy if trained
- Call 000

**Unconscious**

- Clear airway
- Follow [[Responding to an Emergency Situation#Basic Life Support|Basic Life Support]] flow chart
- Call 000
